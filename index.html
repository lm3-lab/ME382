A note on LLM usage. These are meant to be illustrative examples of some key concepts in ME382 which will be expanded over time. All code is generated by an LLM under human guidance; we provide a human/LLM attribution for each section below. The intention, as time allows, is for the amount of LLM text to converge to zero.

Demonstrations

Lens, eutectic, peritectic

Why does an alloy melt lower than either metal it is made from? Slide one number and watch a plain freezing range turn into a eutectic, with the energy curves behind it shown live.

Human (Tom): formulae for free energy model, spec for interface and explanatory text.
LLM: free energy to phase diagram conversion script, interactive interface, images. Open the demonstration → Dislocations and the strength of a crystal

Why a real crystal yields far below the strength its bonds imply, and how one hard particle in the way takes that strength back. Two molecular dynamics movies in BCC iron, each beside its own stress–strain curve.

Human: description of deliverable, choice of model system, and instructions for validity / interpretation claims.
LLM: raw data and video generation, most of the text at present. Watch the demonstrations →
